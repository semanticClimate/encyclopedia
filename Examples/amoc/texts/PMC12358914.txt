Correction for “Weakening AMOC reduces ocean carbon uptake and increases the social cost of carbon,” by Felix Schaumann and Eduardo Alastrué de Asenjo, which published February 24, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2419543122 ( Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122 , e2419543122 ). The authors note that there was a typographical error in the “Economic damage estimation” subsection of the “Impacts of the AMOC Carbon Feedback” section in the article’s Materials and Methods . The authors explain that on page 8, right column, 4th full paragraph, line 5, “A φ value of 1 means there is no persistence; a φ value of 1 means there is full persistence” should instead appear as “A φ value of 1 means there is no persistence; a φ value of 0 means there is full persistence.” The authors are correcting the text to ensure that there is clarity in the understanding and interpretability of the applied method. The online version has been corrected.
